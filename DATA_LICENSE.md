# External data

The salary extension uses public data from OverTheCap, nflverse, the Kaggle
current-contract snapshot, Deloitte, UEFA, club annual reports, and official
league tables. Sources, coverage, and scope notes are in
`docs/EXTERNAL_DATA.md` and `docs/SOCCER_DATA.md`.

- Raw external downloads are excluded from this repository by `.gitignore`.
- The derived 96-row team-season panel contains aggregate facts and is
  redistributed with citations; raw source pages are not redistributed.
- The Kaggle current-contract snapshot is Apache-2.0. The nflverse data is
  public and cited. OverTheCap, Deloitte, UEFA, and club publications are cited
  as sources; consult each source's terms before redistributing raw content.
- The soccer table is illustrative; accounting scopes and season windows differ
  by club and source.
